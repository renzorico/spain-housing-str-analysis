The 31-night line

In Catalonia, a stay of 31 days or fewer in a tourist flat needs a tourist licence. Longer stays are seasonal lets, under different rules. This page looks at where Airbnb listings sit against that line, and against the other housing rules in force. It reports what the data shows, and what it cannot show. It takes no position on the rules.

Where would you draw the line?

Each bar is the share of active listings asking for that minimum stay. Move the line and see how many listings sit on each side. In Barcelona, they pile up at exactly 31 and 32 nights.

Why 31? A tourist stay is “a continuous period of 31 days or fewer” (Decret 75/2020, art. 221-1). Whether a 31-night booking counts as 31 days or fewer is ambiguous, so 31 is shown as its own boundary value. Other regions draw their lines differently.
